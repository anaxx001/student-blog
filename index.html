About the blog

A little about me

Nobody told me what radiography would actually feel like before I got in. To be honest, the profession is so niche and radiographers are so rare that I knew nothing about them until I got admitted. Good decision or bad one? Time will tell.

This blog is just a space where I share my little thoughts on base courses like anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, and my major profession.

I'm a 200-level radiography student, and this blog is my notebook in public. I'll be writing about what I'm learning, how I study for it, what clinicals are actually like, and the things nobody says out loud about being a health science student in a Nigerian university.

I'm not an expert. That's kind of the point. If you're a health science student too, you'll probably recognize a lot of this. And if you're not, maybe you'll see the profession a little differently.

Three posts a week, more or less depending on how the week treats me. Exam weeks excused. Let's see how far this goes.
